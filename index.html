About

I teach small models what the giants know, then put them to work.

I'm Dhruv, a software developer working where modern AI meets the enterprise back office. My core craft is knowledge distillation: transferring the capability of massive models into compact ones that run fast, private, and anywhere.

At Metavision Technology I ship that thinking into real businesses, building AI and agentic workflows on top of Tally and ERP systems used by logistics, biotech and finance companies. Legacy systems hold the knowledge; modern AI passes it on. The fresco above is the job description: one hand passing the spark to another.
